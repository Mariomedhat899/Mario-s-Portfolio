SmartDocQna

I was paying to ask questions of my own notes. So I stopped — and made a thing that answers from a PDF, offline, for free.

ASP.NET CoreRAGOllamaVector Search
2026

IMS-Backend

A friend's shop kept running out of stock by surprise. The only feature he uses is the email that says "buy more, today."

ASP.NET CoreEF CoreSQL ServerIdentitySMTP
2026
  • Inventory API: products & categories, transaction tracking, and a Low Stock Alerts system that watches thresholds so a human doesn't have to.
  • Auth via ASP.NET Core Identity — role-based, with a seeder that sets up admin/user on first run.
  • CSV import/export for bulk work, plus reports that a shop owner would actually read.
  • SMTP notifications through IEmailService — the owner gets the email before he notices the gap.
